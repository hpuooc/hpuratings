Four things High Point University guarantees that Tampa does not

  1. An internship, guaranteed

    Finish the five-step career program and you will have an internship. Tampa makes no such promise.

  2. Your degree, finished in 4 years

    A published four-year plan, a Success Coach, and a checkpoint every year so you graduate on time.

  3. A tuition-free summer abroad

    Take a four-credit Maymester course overseas and HPU covers the tuition. At Tampa you pay tuition.

  4. A tuition-free master's degree

    Get a master's degree at HPU without paying tuition. Eleven programs qualify.

Basketball

NCAA

Back‑to‑back Big South championships. Back‑to‑back NCAA Tournament bids. And in March, a tournament win over Wisconsin.

High Point University knocked out Wisconsin, 83 to 82, on a layup with fourteen seconds left. The men finished 31 and 5, more wins than any Big South team has ever posted in a season. The women went 27 and 6 and took the conference title too. When the Panthers made the tournament, the university flew more than 200 students to Portland to watch.
